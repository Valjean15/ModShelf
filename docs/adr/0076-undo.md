# 76. Undo

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-06

## Context

Removing a mod from the library asked first; unwatching, pinning, unpinning, skipping an update and removing a note
didn't, and none could be taken back but by doing the opposite, which lost what went with it: an unwatched mod's
updates, a forgotten mod's records, updates, pin and note.

The plan (step 16): after Forget, Unwatch, Skip, Pin, Unpin and removing a note, a bar at the bottom says what happened,
with **Undo**, for about ten seconds; undo puts the library back as it was; deleting a file isn't undone, since the file
is gone, and keeps asking first. The approved mockups (step 8): the bar at the bottom, one at a time, about ten seconds.

The human chose (2026-11-06) to put undo **in Application**, where the command line and the MCP server could use it
later.

## Decision

### Application

- `IModLibrary.RestoreAsync(LibraryChange change, CancellationToken)`, a public addition the human approved: given the
  change a method returned, it puts the mod back as it was before it, with its records, updates, pin, skipped updates
  and note, in one transaction, and publishes what it did like any change. Nothing on disk is touched, and the site
  isn't asked.
- Its files are the library's as they are now: a download recorded since stays, and a file deleted since stays deleted,
  with the updates that go with them.
- It refuses, changing nothing (`InvalidOperationException`, saying why), a mod taken out by the change and back in the
  library since, or one the change left in and gone since; and (`ArgumentException`) a change that added the mod.
- Logged (event 629). The recent activity keeps a removal undone: it lists what happened to files and mods, and the
  files didn't move.

### Presentation

- `UndoBarViewModel` (`ViewModels/Undo/`), a singleton the main window shows (`MainWindowViewModel.Undo`, a new
  constructor parameter): `Offer(said, change)` shows what happened with Undo, in place of what it showed; it closes
  after `ShownFor` (ten seconds, from the `TimeProvider`), with its ×, or with **Undo**, which calls `RestoreAsync`. A
  refusal shows "Couldn't undo: …" for as long; anything else is logged (event 957) and said.
- What offers it, once the library has made the change, and only when the change changed something:
  - a Library row's **Remove from library**, **Unwatch**, **Pin at this version**, **Unpin**, and a note emptied (not
    one added or changed);
  - a mod page's **Remove from library** and unwatching;
  - the Updates screen's **Skip**.
- **Remove from library no longer asks first**, on a row or a page, as undo takes the question's place; its label loses
  its ellipsis, and the bar says the files stay. The question's strings are gone. Deleting a file still asks.
- `LibraryServices`, `ModPageServices` and `UpdatesServices` carry the bar; the Library, Browse and Updates screens take
  it.

### Desktop

- `MainWindow`: over the screen shown, at its bottom, `Border.undoBar` (at most 560 wide, raised, with a shadow): the
  message, read out as it changes (`AutomationProperties.LiveSetting`), **Undo** while it can, and a × to close it.

## Consequences

- The library's interface gains a method; its two fakes follow.
- Undoing after an update check meanwhile puts back the updates as they were before the change; the next check finds
  them again.
- A completed download that forgetting cleared from the queue stays cleared: its file is the library's again.
- The command line and the MCP server don't offer undo yet.

## Testing

- The library: a forget undone with its files, updates, pin, skip and note, nothing on disk touched, the site not asked,
  the change published and logged; an unwatch undone with its updates; a pin, an unpin, a skip and a note removed
  undone; the files kept as they are now; a mod back since, or gone since, left as it is; a change that added the mod
  refused.
- The bar: nothing at first; ten seconds, closing on the UI thread; an end that waited for the UI thread leaving a newer
  change; a newer change taking the place of the one offered;
  undoing, then closing; closing forgets; a refusal said for ten seconds; a failure logged and said; a change offered
  while undoing staying offered.
- The rows, the page and the Updates screen offering it after each action, and undoing through it; removing without a
  question; watching and changing a note offering nothing.
- 26 deliberate bugs, all caught. One got through at first, a mod's page naming the mod removed by the site's name
  rather than the library's, as the tests gave both the same name: a test now gives them two.
- Looked at here, on Linux: unwatching on a mod's page, the bar at the bottom; removing it, then **Undo**, the mod back,
  watched, with its file and its update.
- By hand: `docs/manual-checklist-desktop.md` 8.3b and 9.5.
